# Jainism

Jainism is an ancient Indian religion centered on non-violence, truth, non-attachment, and disciplined spiritual practice. Its tradition honors twenty-four Tirthankaras, enlightened teachers who show the path toward liberation from the cycle of rebirth. The best-known Tirthankara in the current era is Mahavira, who lived in the 6th century BCE and helped shape the teachings followed by Jain communities today.
